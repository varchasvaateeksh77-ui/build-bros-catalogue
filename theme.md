# Four Curated Product Themes Specification (`theme.md`)

Each theme in the Build Bros Catalogue is not just a colour swap—it has its own **layout structure, typographic voice, border radius, and atmosphere** so a guest immediately feels the venue's identity.

---

## Theme 01 · `Classy Elegant` (*L'Atelier Doré*)
- **Target Venues**: Fine dining, wine bars, candlelit bistros, tasting rooms.
- **Palette**:
  - Canvas: `#0E0B09` (Obsidian Walnut)
  - Surface: `#17120F` (Smoked Espresso)
  - Text: `#F5EFE6` (Warm Silk Ivory) · Muted: `#A6998A`
  - Accent Spark: `#C89D54` (Brushed Antique Gold)
- **Typography**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Display, italic/semibold) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (UI).
- **Menu Layout Personality**: Centered, single-column **Editorial Tasting Menu** with generous vertical spacing, fine gold horizontal rules between courses, and understated `+ Add` buttons.

---

## Theme 02 · `Minimalist` (*Kōbō Nordic*)
- **Target Venues**: Specialty coffee roasteries, Scandinavian sourdough bakeries, daylight brunch cafes.
- **Palette**:
  - Canvas: `#FAF9F5` (Warm Alabaster Paper)
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Ceramic)
  - Text: `#141413` (Charcoal Ink) · Muted: `#6E6A63`
  - Accent Spark: `#1E3F2B` (Nordic Pine)
- **Typography**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean architectural sans) + `Google Sans Code` (Tabular prices).
- **Menu Layout Personality**: Clean **2-Column Architectural Grid** bordered by `1px` stone hairlines, abundant white space, and pill-free typographic category tabs.

---

## Theme 03 · `Street Style` (*Block 84 Smash*)
- **Target Venues**: Smash burger joints, late-night fry counters, food trucks, taco bars.
- **Palette**:
  - Canvas: `#111111` (Asphalt Night)
  - Surface: `#1B1B1B` (Raw Concrete)
  - Text: `#F4F2ED` (Chalk White) · Muted: `#9E9A90`
  - Accent Spark: `#FF4D00` (High-Voltage Flame Orange)
- **Typography**: `Big Shoulders Display` (Uppercase condensed poster type) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body).
- **Menu Layout Personality**: Bold **Poster-Grid Cards** with oversized uppercase dish titles, high-contrast orange price tags, and fast one-tap `+ ADD` buttons.

---

## Theme 04 · `Artisanal Terracotta` (*Forno Terra*)
- **Target Venues**: Wood-fired Neapolitan pizzerias, Mediterranean courtyards, farm-to-table trattorias.
- **Palette**:
  - Canvas: `#F7F1E7` (Sunlit Kiln Clay)
  - Surface: `#FFFDF9` (Warm Flour)
  - Text: `#231811` (Roasted Olive Wood) · Muted: `#7A6352`
  - Accent Spark: `#C84B24` (Wood-Fired Terracotta)
- **Typography**: `Fraunces` (Soft optical serif) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body).
- **Menu Layout Personality**: Warm, organic **Courtyard Menu Cards** (`20px` radius) with subtle clay borders, wood-fired prep time callouts, and inviting terracotta action buttons.
